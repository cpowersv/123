📺 Cast Audivue to your TV

Works with Apple TV, AirPlay-2 TVs, and Roku.

Tap the 📺 Cast button in Safari on iPhone, iPad, or Mac and pick your TV — only the visuals are sent, full-screen and clean.

  1. Newer Roku (AirPlay 2): on the Roku, enable Settings → Apple AirPlay and HomeKit. Then on your iPhone/iPad/Mac, open Screen Mirroring / AirPlay and pick the Roku.
  2. Android / Windows Roku: Roku Settings → System → Screen mirroring → then cast from your device's Smart View / Project.
  1. iPhone / iPad: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → your TV.
  2. Mac: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → your TV.
  3. Then press ⛶ TV here for fullscreen — the screen is kept awake automatically.

Tip: Screen Mirroring carries the music too if it's playing on the same device. With direct AirPlay, keep the audio playing on your device.
