How it works

  1. 1

    Answer four questions

    Where you live, how old your kids are, weekday or weekend, and whether you want them along. That’s the whole signup.

  2. 2

    We put you in a group

    Eight to twelve moms near you, with kids around the same age. A real person does the matching — you’re not swiping through anybody.

  3. 3

    You show up

    First Saturday of every month, same time, somewhere easy. It’s already on the calendar. We send the reminder. You just come.

What this isn’t

  • Swiping through other moms. Nobody gets judged by a photo.
  • Coffee alone with one stranger. If it’s awkward, there are ten other people at the table.
  • Another group chat that dies in a week. The date is fixed before anyone has to volunteer.
  • Guilt. No streaks, no reminders about what you missed, no to-do list. The whole point is the day you don’t do any of that.

It’s one standing plan a month, with moms who get it.

The obvious questions

Can I bring my kids?
Some groups are kid-friendly, some are strictly kid-free. You pick when you sign up, and we match you accordingly. Nobody shows up to a surprise.
What does it cost?
Nothing to join. You buy your own coffee.
Where do you meet?
Public places only — a café, a park, a patio. Never someone’s house until the group knows each other and decides otherwise.
What if I can’t make one?
Then you skip it and come the next month. There’s always a next month. That’s the entire idea.
Is it safe?
Every member verifies a phone number, groups meet in public, and each group has a host who can remove anyone. We’re small and careful on purpose.
